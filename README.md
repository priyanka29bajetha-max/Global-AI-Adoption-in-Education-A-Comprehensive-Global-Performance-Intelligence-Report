# Global-AI-Adoption-in-Education-A-Comprehensive-Global-Performance-Intelligence-Report
A comprehensive report on global AI adoption in education and its impact on learning and performance.

## Project Overview
**Global AI in Education** is a data visualization and analysis project that explores the adoption and use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in education across different countries and regions.
The project examines how AI adoption varies among students, teachers, and schools, while also exploring factors such as internet penetration, education development, urban-rural differences, gender gaps, AI tools, government policies, and curriculum integration.
The analysis covers the period **2015–2026**.
---
## Objectives
The main objectives of this project are to:
* Analyze AI adoption trends in education from 2015 to 2026.
* Compare AI adoption across countries and regions.
* Compare AI usage among students, teachers, and schools.
* Examine the relationship between AI adoption and internet penetration.
* Explore the relationship between education development and AI adoption.
* Identify urban-rural and gender-related differences in AI usage.
* Compare the use of major AI tools among students and teachers.
* Examine government AI policies and AI integration into education curricula.
* Present the findings through interactive Tableau visualizations and dashboards.
